Welcome, Stranger

You have found the OFFICIAL home page of the GRAVY BLOODS MOTORCYCLE CLUB. We are a riding club out of the valley, founded in 1994 in the parking lot of a diner that no longer exists, over a plate of biscuits that people still talk about.

We ride. We wrench. We show up. If your bike dies on the shoulder at 2am and you have our number, somebody is coming. That is the whole club, right there.
