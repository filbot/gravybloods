☠ OUR COLORS ☠

What It Means

The skull is for the ones who rode ahead of us. The fire is for the ones still riding. The colors have not changed since 1994 and they are not going to.

The patch is earned, not bought. Do not ask to buy one. The answer is no, and Tuna will be the one telling you.

Where The Name Came From

1994. Diner parking lot. Six bikes and an argument about whether the brown gravy or the sausage gravy was better. It got heated. Somebody said "we're gonna be bleeding gravy over this" and that was that.

People assume the name means something tougher. We let them.
