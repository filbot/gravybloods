Rules of the Run

  1. Full tank before you show up. We are not waiting on you.
  2. Staggered formation. You know the one. If you don't, ask before we roll.
  3. Hand signals get passed back down the line. Every time.
  4. Nobody gets left on the side of the road. Ever.
  5. The Road Captain's call is final while we are moving.
  6. Argue at the diner, not at 60 miles an hour.
